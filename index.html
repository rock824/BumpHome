Bump Home!

Race your pawns around the board, bump your rivals, and get everybody home.
Local pass-and-play.
Roll 6 to leave Start. Exact roll required to reach Home.

How to play: Each player has 3 pawns. Roll a 6 to move a pawn out of Start. After that, move a pawn forward by the number rolled. Land on an opponent and they get bumped back to Start. Landing on one of your own pawns is not allowed. You must roll the exact number needed to reach Home. Roll a 6 and you get another roll. First player to get all 3 pawns Home wins.
